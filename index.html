Courses

Twenty-nine fixed routes. Mix ducking, smash floors, stairs, and upper or lower paths. Reach the visible finish flag and earn up to three stars.

Five worlds are available from the start. Save earned shards for Aurora Drift, Emberfall, and ten new cosmetic effects. Complete 73 career challenges and rotating daily goals. Core play works offline.
