Courses

Nine fixed routes. Try ducking, smash-through floors, and upper or lower paths in the new course. Reach the visible finish flag and earn up to three stars.

Forest, desert, ocean, alpine, and night-sky worlds each have their own music. Collect shards, unlock trails, and complete daily and career challenges. Core play works offline.
