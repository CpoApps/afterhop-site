Privacy policy

AFTERHOP saves your progress on your device and can use optional Apple services. You do not need a CpoApps account to play.

Effective date: November 16, 2026
Publisher: CpoApps
Applies to: AFTERHOP 4.1 gameplay betas. Build 5 leaves advertising inactive; builds 6 and 7 enable consent requests and Google test ads. Live advertising remains disabled.

Progress on your device

AFTERHOP stores scores, course stars, challenge progress, earned shards, cosmetic items, and settings on your device. A random installation identifier distinguishes progress written by different installations when saves are merged. It is not a hardware identifier or advertising identifier. Core gameplay works offline.

Optional Apple services

iCloud progress

If you are signed in to iCloud and AFTERHOP has access, the game sends progress records and the installation identifier to Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. These records let devices using the same iCloud account merge progress. CpoApps does not operate an iCloud replacement or receive a copy on its own servers. On an iCloud account change, the game asks before bringing local progress into the newly selected account. Delivery is controlled by Apple and may take time.

Game Center

If you connect Game Center, AFTERHOP displays your Game Center nickname and uses a game-scoped player identifier locally to keep submissions associated with the signed-in player. When the relevant Game Center resources are available, scores and achievement progress are sent to Apple. CpoApps does not export your Game Center nickname or identifier to a separate developer server or advertising service. Your profile and score visibility are controlled by Game Center and your Apple privacy settings. Some resources may be unavailable in the beta.

Purchases

Optional ad removal, when available, is processed through Apple’s StoreKit. AFTERHOP checks signed transaction entitlements to determine whether it is owned and to restore it. CpoApps does not receive your payment card details. Live purchases may not be configured in a gameplay beta.

Apple handles its services under Apple’s privacy policy. For more detail, see Game Center & Privacy.

Advertising in the current beta

Builds 6 and 7 use Google’s User Messaging Platform to refresh consent information when the app launches. Any required consent form appears at a retry break. A privacy-options button is available in Settings when Google requires it. Google receives requests for consent information and any choices submitted through its forms. Advertising is skipped when the SDK does not permit ad requests or ad removal is owned.

When ad requests are permitted, builds 6 and 7 can initialize Google Mobile Ads and request Google-provided demonstration interstitials between runs. These test creatives do not use the production ad unit or generate advertising revenue. Test ads still involve Google SDK and network processing. Google’s SDK may process device or app identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions, crash information, and performance data for advertising, diagnostics, and analytics. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and advertising privacy information. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission in this beta.

Live advertising remains disabled and requires a further policy and App Store disclosure review for the production configuration. Non-personalized or test ads must not be treated as data-free. If you decline or cannot complete a required consent flow, core gameplay remains available.

Developer servers and permissions

The current app has no CpoApps-operated gameplay, account, analytics, or crash-reporting server. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or device location. AFTERHOP does not sell gameplay records or share them with an advertising broker.

TestFlight and support requests

Apple may process crash information, screenshots, and beta feedback through TestFlight and share feedback with the developer under your TestFlight settings and Apple’s terms. If you contact CpoApps through our GitHub support tracker, GitHub processes the information under GitHub’s privacy statement. CpoApps can read the request and its attachments to answer it and investigate the problem. Do not include private information: GitHub issues are public and may remain visible until removed through GitHub’s controls.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Local progress remains while the app’s data is retained on your device or in a backup. Removing the app removes its local application data, but does not by itself delete iCloud progress, Game Center records, purchases, backups, or feedback held separately.
  • You can manage AFTERHOP’s iCloud access and Game Center sign-in through iOS or iPadOS Settings. Disabling sync stops further synchronization; it does not by itself erase previously stored cloud records.
  • You can use Apple’s controls for Apple Account and service data. CpoApps has no separate developer account database to delete and does not have your Apple Account password.
  • For a request about app progress or feedback, contact CpoApps through the privacy tracker. We can explain the available device/service controls or handle data that you sent directly to us. Do not post sensitive information; start with a general description.
  • Support information is kept while needed to answer and investigate requests. Public GitHub posts may remain accessible under GitHub’s retention and deletion controls. You can ask for information you provided to be removed, subject to the platform’s tools and any necessary recordkeeping.

Children

AFTERHOP is a general-audience game and is not submitted in Apple’s Kids category. Core play does not ask for personal information. Optional Apple services follow the signed-in account’s settings. Advertising and age-related settings will be reviewed before any live advertising is introduced.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, forms that submit to CpoApps, or embedded tracking services. GitHub may process ordinary hosting request information under its own privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to that provider’s service.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when AFTERHOP’s data practices change, including before live ads are enabled. For questions or requests, open an AFTERHOP privacy request with CpoApps. Please do not include passwords, Apple Account details, payment information, or other sensitive information.
