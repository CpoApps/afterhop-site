Controls

How do I jump and use the second hop?

Tap and release to jump. A quick tap in the air performs the second hop. Keep taps short if you want to hop while airborne.

How do holding and crouching work?

Hold on the ground to crouch, then release to make a normal jump. Builds 7–9 also run 15% faster while crouched on the ground. Holding longer does not increase jump height. In builds 7–9, hold in the air for 0.22 seconds to dive (0.30 seconds in earlier 4.1 betas). Keep holding through your landing to stay crouched, then release on the ground to jump. Once a dive has started, releasing before landing does not use the second hop.

Can I still swipe down?

Yes. Swipe down to dive immediately. Diving accelerates your fall; you still need a platform underneath you.

Runs and progress

How do I play Jump. Duck. Smash.?

Build 8 introduced this course. In build 9, ducking, smash floors, upper/lower paths, and stair sections also appear in Endless, Daily Drift, and generated courses. Hold while grounded to duck under branches; a missed duck causes a brief stumble. Dive onto wooden floors marked with downward arrows to break through to a safe lower landing. Use your second hop for the upper path and more shards, or follow the wider lower path. Both paths lead to the checkered flag. All existing courses remain available with their saved rewards.

Where do Courses end?

Every Course has a fixed route and a visible finish flag on the last island. Land and reach the flag to finish. Two extra stars reward the goals shown before the run; these goals count when you also finish. Build 9 has 29 courses and a Next route button after finishing. In Jump. Duck. Smash., duck under three branches and smash two floors for the bonus stars.

What can I save shards for?

Build 9 keeps the original ten trails and adds ten effects priced from 1,500 to 16,000 shards. Aurora Drift and Emberfall cost 5,000 and 10,000 earned shards for selection in Endless and Daily Drift. Existing ownership stays intact; new purchases use game shards, not real money. There are 73 career challenges plus rotating daily goals.

When does Daily Drift change?

A new shared seed starts each day at midnight UTC. Your device’s local midnight can be at a different time.

How does iCloud progress work?

Progress saves on your device. When signed in to iCloud with access enabled for AFTERHOP, the game can synchronize progress through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Use the same iCloud account on each device and allow time for delivery. A sync request is not a guarantee of an immediate update. If you change iCloud accounts, choose whether to bring local progress into the new account.

Why is Game Center unavailable?

Check your Game Center sign-in in iOS or iPadOS Settings and your connection. Game Center and iCloud use separate sign-ins. Rankings and achievements also need to be available for your build. The current beta may not have all Game Center resources configured. You can keep playing offline.

Sound and devices

Can I change the music and effects?

Open Settings in AFTERHOP to change music, volume, effects, and haptics separately. Silent Mode silences the game’s music. In build 9, use Restart music if audio stays silent after an interruption. Check the music switch, volume, and audio output too. The two earned worlds reuse Alpine Dawn and Sunstone music.

Which devices and orientations work?

AFTERHOP supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Build 9 supports portrait and landscape on both iPhone and iPad. Earlier betas support landscape on iPad only.

Ads and purchases

Does this beta show ads?

Build 5 has advertising inactive. Builds 6–9 can show Google test ads at voluntary retry or next-route breaks after any required consent. They are eligible every fourth completed run, after a run lasting at least 12 seconds, with at least three minutes between ads. Builds 6–8 prepare at the first eligible break. Build 9 preloads earlier whenever Google permits ad requests, and Settings includes Preview test ad with a readiness message. Rejecting privacy choices may limit or prevent ads; the app never overrides that choice. A missing ad may also mean there is no fill or an ad has not loaded. Test ads produce no advertising revenue; live ads remain disabled. If consent or an ad is unavailable, you can keep playing. The planned free release may show interstitial ads between runs.

How do I restore ad removal?

If ad removal is available in your build, use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple Account that bought it. Apple displays the regional price before any purchase. The intended removal is one permanent non-consumable purchase, with no subscription. An unavailable product does not prevent playing; the beta may not have live purchases configured.

How can I report an inappropriate ad?

If a future build displays an inappropriate ad, contact CpoApps with the advertiser’s name and what happened. You may attach a screenshot after removing personal information. Do not tap an ad you believe is unsafe.
